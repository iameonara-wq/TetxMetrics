Frequently Asked Questions

How long should an SEO meta description be?

An SEO meta description should typically be between 155 and 160 characters. Search engines like Google truncate snippets longer than this limit (around 600 pixels on desktop), so keeping it within this range ensures your entire description is visible in search results without being cut off.

How many words is a 5-minute speech?

A 5-minute speech is typically around 650 words when spoken at an average, conversational pace of 130 words per minute. Here is a quick reference table:

Speech LengthEstimated Words (at 130 WPM)
1 minute130 words
3 minutes390 words
5 minutes650 words
10 minutes1,300 words

Where does LinkedIn cut off text with "see more"?

LinkedIn typically truncates posts and shows the "...see more" link at around 210 characters on desktop and mobile feeds. To ensure your most important hook or call-to-action is visible without requiring a click, place it within the first 210 characters.

What is the difference between reading time and speaking time?

Silent reading time is generally faster, averaging 200 to 250 words per minute for most adults. Speaking time is slower to allow for articulation, natural pauses, and audience comprehension, averaging around 130 to 150 words per minute depending on the setting and speaker.
